CASE ASSIGNMENT

Use Agoda's API to design hotel cancellation flow

We are building an Android app to book hotels online. Since as a young start-up it is impossible to onboard many hotels on our own, we will be using Agoda's APIs to launch a product.

When users browse through details of a hotel, we show them all the details like - hotel name, hotel images, hotel description, hotel address, amenities, price, etc as passed by Agoda to us in a feed. Once a user selects, we book the hotel on behalf of the user by passing on the details further along to Agoda. As a PM, you have been asked to create wireframes for a new use case - cancellation of an existing booking.

By understanding the APIs in the link, create mobile wireframes for hotel cancellation flow using Agoda's APIs

- https://partners.agoda.com/DeveloperPortal/APIDoc/GettingStarted
- https://partners.agoda.com/DeveloperPortal/APIDoc/SearchAPI

Assignment due date: Oct 25th, 2026 - 11:59pm

Team Strength: 4-5 members only. nothing more, nothing less.

Expected Submission:

- Upto 11+2 slides/pages
- Workflow/Flowchart diagram that illustrates the Information Architecture and flow of logic+information
- Each wireframe to include Design team notes (UI/UX), Developer notes (API usage) and Business team notes
- Product Note on the overall flow (1-2 pager)
- You can make your own assumptions as long as they're clearly stated
